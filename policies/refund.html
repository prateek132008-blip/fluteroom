Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains the terms under which payments made to The Flute Room for flute classes may be refunded. Please read this policy carefully before enrolling.

1. General Policy

Because seats and instructor time are reserved for you as soon as you enroll, payments made towards any plan (Monthly, 3 Months, or 6 Months) are generally non-refundable once your first class has been scheduled or attended.

2. Before Your First Class

If you have not yet attended any class, you may request a full refund within 48 hours of payment by contacting us at the email or WhatsApp number below.

3. After Your First Class

  • No refunds will be issued once a class has been attended, except where required by applicable law.
  • Plan duration cannot be paused or extended for missed classes unless agreed in writing with the team.

4. Payment Errors

If you were charged incorrectly (e.g. duplicate payment, wrong amount), contact us immediately with your payment ID and we will investigate and correct the issue.

5. How Refunds Are Processed

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method via Razorpay within 5–10 business days.

6. Contact

For refund requests, email prateek132008@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp, including your Student Code and payment ID.
