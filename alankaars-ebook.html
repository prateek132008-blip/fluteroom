Why alankaars

Songs get easier when your fundamentals are strong

Learning song after song from notation or videos works — until you want to play something no one has written down. The missing piece is usually not more songs, it's fundamentals: hearing a swar and knowing where your fingers should go.

An alankaar is a short, repeating pattern of swars. Practising them every day builds exactly that connection between your ear and your fingers.
