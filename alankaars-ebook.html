Getting Started

What is an Alankaar?

An alankaar is a structured sequence of swars (notes) that you practice repeatedly on your flute. Instead of jumping straight into songs, alankaars give you a simple, repeatable pattern to train your fingers and ears together, one step at a time.

30 Alankaras for Flute brings together 30 such patterns that you can practice progressively, alongside your regular flute practice.

01

Finger Coordination & Speed

Repeating a pattern helps build the muscle memory behind smoother, more confident finger movement over time.

02

Smoother Swar Transitions

Regular practice helps your fingers move between notes with less hesitation and more control.

03

Ear Training & Swar Identification

Playing the same patterns often trains your ear to recognize swars, an important part of overall musical understanding.
