Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how The Flute Room collects, uses, and protects your information when you use this website and enroll in classes.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name, email address, WhatsApp number, and age submitted via our enrollment form
  • Playing level, preferred time slot, and selected plan
  • Payment confirmation details (Payment ID) from Razorpay — we do not store your card, UPI, or bank details
  • Basic browsing data via Meta Pixel for advertising measurement

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule and deliver your flute classes
  • To generate and send your Student Code
  • To communicate with you about your enrollment via email or WhatsApp
  • To measure the effectiveness of our advertising (via Meta Pixel)

3. Data Storage

Enrollment data is stored securely in a private Google Sheet accessible only to authorized staff.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Razorpay for payment processing, Google Apps Script/Sheets for data storage, and Meta Pixel for advertising analytics. Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the email below.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, email prateek132008@gmail.com.
