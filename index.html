Curriculum

Your roadmap from beginner to advanced

A structured journey — every stage builds directly on the last, so nothing feels like a jump.

Beginner

Foundations & First Sounds

Blowing technique, posture, producing your first notes, and understanding octaves and basic sargam.

Beginner

Practice & Rhythm

Alankars for fluency, metronome practice, and the theory behind scales and different flute sizes.

Intermediate

Ornamentation Techniques

Meend, gamak, kan swars, murki, khatka, and zamzamma — the expressive techniques that shape a phrase.

Intermediate

Articulation & Tonguing

Komal swars, chromatic scale practice, and tatkaar (tonguing) exercises for cleaner note separation.

Application

Songs, Tunes & Expression

Bollywood songs, popular flute tunes, and devotional bhajans — combining technique with real music, plus scale shifting and sound control.
